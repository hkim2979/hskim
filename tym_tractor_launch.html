TYM Tractor Launch


For the Greenest Grass, Think Red. Think TYM.


A great harvest begins long before the first seed is sown, with the machine you trust to get the job done.

For farmers and landowners, a tractor is far more than equipment. It is the workhorse that guards the field before sunrise, stays through the longest days, and delivers when a whole year’s work is on the line. So when TYM unveiled its new tractor, I began shaping a campaign as dependable and powerful as the machine itself.

I led the entire launch campaign, from concept to execution. I produced the product imagery, launch videos, print materials including brochures, and website banners, and I oversaw the photo shoot that captured the tractor at work in the field. By weaving the “Think Red” message consistently through every touchpoint, I created a campaign that felt unified, from the first image a customer encountered to the brochure they held in their hands.
